Fire lookout vacancies

Seasonal lookout assignments involve prolonged periods alone in remote towers, repetitive observation, radio reporting and basic station maintenance. Applicants should be comfortable with isolation and changing mountain weather.

Staffed observation tower overlooking the central Blackpine drainage. Daily duties include fire-weather observations, smoke plotting, public radio contact and station maintenance. Access is by rough service road followed by 1.1 miles on foot.
